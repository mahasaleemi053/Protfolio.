Client Campaign

Clearwell Dermatology — Adult Acne Multi-Session Treatment Program

Dermatology & Medical Aesthetic $600–$1,200 Packages
Target Audience:

Warm traffic, adults 20–30, burned out on products that overpromised and skeptical of upsells.

The Offer Structure:

Medical-grade facials, topicals, laser therapy. $150–$350 single session, $600–$1,200 for 4–6 session package.

Ad Script 01

Ad 1: "The Five Minute Visit"

Hook (0:00–0:03)

"I used to give patients five minutes and a prescription. I don't do that anymore."

Problem Agitation (0:03–0:18)

"Early in my career, that's what a packed schedule looked like. Write the script, move to next room. That's not good medicine, it's just fast medicine."

Unique Mechanism (0:18–0:32)

"Now I walk through why your acne keeps returning before I treat anything. We build a 6 to 8 week plan that adjusts as skin heals."

Call to Action (0:32–0:42)

"If your last dermatologist visit felt rushed, come see us. Link below."

Why it works: Reframes rushed appointments as something the doctor corrected, building instant trust through vulnerability.
Ad Script 02

Ad 2: "The Upsell"

Hook (0:00–0:03)

"I'm a dermatologist, and I will not point at your pores to sell you a product line."

Problem Agitation (0:03–0:16)

"I've had patients come after MedSpa visits where an esthetician handed them a $600 shopping list. That's a sales pitch wearing a lab coat."

Call to Action (0:30–0:40)

"Book a consultation and see how a session runs without the pitch at the end."
