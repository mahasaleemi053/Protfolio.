Most clinics don't have an attention problem.

A patient watches the Reel. They click the ad. They land on the page, and for a moment, they're interested. Then, somewhere between interest and booking, they leave, and nobody on the team can point to exactly where it happened.

That gap is rarely a traffic problem. It's a trust problem, sitting in the words between the click and the calendar. The hook that doesn't hold. The page that doesn't answer the objection already forming in the patient's head. The follow-up that never comes, so a warm lead goes quiet.

I write copy that closes that specific gap: ad and Reel scripts that pre-qualify the right patient, VSLs that pre-sell the treatment before the consult, and email sequences that win back the ones who didn't convert the first time.

One line from my own positioning, worth stating plainly: I aim for 15 to 20% more booked appointments within 30 days. That's a target I write toward, not a guarantee attached to every clinic or every campaign.

Four services, one system

Each service does a specific job in the same journey, from a stranger's attention to a booked patient.

Ads

Meta Ads & Ad Copy

Direct-response ad scripts built to stop the scroll, speak to a specific pain point, and pull the right prospect toward an inquiry rather than a passive like. Every angle is built around an objection your audience is already carrying, so the ad disarms it instead of ignoring it.

Reels

Reel & Short-Form Video Scripts

Not captions. Full scripts, structured around a hook that earns the next three seconds, a pain point the viewer recognizes, and a retention arc that carries them to a clear call to action, built for education and persuasion rather than a trending sound.

VSL

VSL Scripts

Long-form video sales letters that build trust, name the real problem, walk through the mechanism behind the offer, handle the objections a skeptical patient hasn't said out loud yet, and move them toward booking without leaning on hype.

Email

Email Sequences

Sequences for the patients who clicked, got interested, and then went quiet. Built to nurture, handle the objections holding them back, and re-open the door to booking without reading like a discount blast.

How I think about the work

Three stages, each with its own job and its own copy.

Pre-Qualify

Ads and Reel scripts that attract the right people and make them want to take the next step, so the leads coming in are already closer to a fit.

Pre-Sell

VSLs and conversion-focused copy that build trust, create desire, and address the objections a prospect would otherwise raise at the consult.

Win Back

Email sequences and follow-up messaging that re-engage the people who were interested but didn't convert the first time around.

Why this approach

  • Every angle starts from a real objection a patient is already carrying, not a generic pain point.
  • Proof is built as a documented process (what week two, four and eight actually look like) rather than a vague promise.
  • Trust is sequenced before the offer, so the pitch lands after the skepticism has somewhere to go.
  • Objections get handled in the reader's own words, not smoothed over with reassurance.
  • Every CTA says exactly what happens next, so nobody has to guess what "learn more" means.
