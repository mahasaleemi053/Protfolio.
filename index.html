Featured Direct-Response Campaign

Full-funnel conversion copywriting for high-ticket aesthetic medicine: Meta Video Ads, a 7-Part Nurture Sequence, and a Full Video Sales Letter (VSL).

Client Campaign

Clearwell Dermatology — Adult Acne Multi-Session Treatment Program

Dermatology & Medical Aesthetic $600–$1,200 Packages
Target Audience:

Warm traffic, adults 20–30, burned out on products that overpromised and skeptical of upsells.

The Offer Structure:

Medical-grade facials, topicals, laser therapy. $150–$350 single session, $600–$1,200 for 4–6 session package.

Ad Script 01

Ad 1: "The Five Minute Visit"

Hook (0:00–0:03)

"I used to give patients five minutes and a prescription. I don't do that anymore."

Problem Agitation (0:03–0:18)

"Early in my career, that's what a packed schedule looked like. Write the script, move to the next room, hope the patient figured out the rest on their own. I'd hear back weeks later that their skin got worse before anyone explained purging was normal. That's not good medicine, it's just fast medicine."

Unique Angle & Mechanism (0:18–0:32)

"Now I sit down with every patient and walk through why their acne keeps returning before I treat anything. We build a six to eight week plan that adjusts as your skin responds, not a script you fill and figure out alone."

Call to Action (0:32–0:42)

"If your last dermatologist visit felt rushed, come see us. I'll actually explain what's happening to your skin before we start treating it."

Why it works: Reframes a common failure point (the rushed appointment) as something the speaker used to do and has since corrected, which builds credibility through admitted imperfection rather than a polished sales claim.
Ad Script 02

Ad 2: "The Upsell"

Hook (0:00–0:03)

"I'm a dermatologist, and I will not point at your pores to sell you a product line."

Problem Agitation (0:03–0:16)

"I've had patients come to me after MedSpa visits where an esthetician listed everything wrong with their skin, then handed them a six hundred dollar shopping list. That's not treatment. That's a sales pitch wearing a lab coat."

Unique Angle & Mechanism (0:16–0:30)

"In my practice, we don't do that. No upsells during your session. We build your treatment plan around your skin, and we work with whatever's already in your routine that's actually helping."

Call to Action (0:30–0:40)

"Book a consultation and see how a session runs without the pitch at the end."

Why it works: Names the exact mechanism of past exploitation (pointing out flaws to sell product) rather than a vague promise of "honesty," which makes the guarantee feel specific and earned.
Ad Script 03

Ad 3: "Still Breaking Out at 26"

Hook (0:00–0:03)

"Patients ask me constantly, why am I still breaking out at 26?"

Problem Agitation (0:03–0:17)

"Adult acne isn't rare, and it's almost never bad luck. Most of the patients I see have spent years stacking cleansers, serums, and prescriptions on top of each other without anyone explaining what their skin actually needed."

Unique Angle & Mechanism (0:17–0:31)

"In most cases, the real issue is a damaged skin barrier from years of harsh products layered together. My program spends the first two weeks calming that barrier before we introduce active treatment at all, which is why results tend to hold instead of relapsing."

Call to Action (0:31–0:41)

"If that sounds like your skin, book a consultation with me. Link below."

Why it works: Externalizes blame away from the patient ("it's almost never bad luck") while still giving a concrete physiological explanation, which relieves shame without sounding dismissive.
Ad Script 04

Ad 4: "The Mirror Check"

Hook (0:00–0:03)

"The patients I see aren't chasing perfect skin. They're exhausted."

Problem Agitation (0:03–0:19)

"Most of them describe the same thing to me. Checking their skin in every mirror they pass, covering up before video calls, second-guessing a photo before they post it. That mental weight is often harder on someone than the acne itself."

Unique Angle & Mechanism (0:19–0:33)

"I'm not going to promise you flawless skin in six weeks. What I can build is a structured plan that calms your skin enough that you stop thinking about it constantly, which is usually what people actually came in for."

Call to Action (0:33–0:43)

"If you're ready to stop checking the mirror, book your first session with me."

Why it works: Explicitly rejects the "flawless skin" promise this audience has learned to distrust, replacing it with the actual emotional relief they desire.
Ad Script 05

Ad 5: "Before You Try Another Serum"

Hook (0:00–0:03)

"Before you buy another forty dollar serum, I want to tell you something as a dermatologist."

Problem Agitation (0:03–0:18)

"I see patients who've spent more treating their acne piecemeal than they realize. A cleanser here, a serum there, a rushed dermatology visit that never really addressed the cause. It adds up, and none of it answers why the breakouts keep coming back."

Unique Angle & Mechanism (0:18–0:32)

"A single session with me runs $150 to $350. If you decide to continue into the full program, that cost applies directly to your package. Nothing wasted while you figure out whether it's right for you."

Call to Action (0:32–0:42)

"Book your first session and I'll walk you through an actual plan for your skin. Link below."

Why it works: Addresses the price objection by reframing it against cumulative spending on failed products, removing financial risk with credit-rollover.
